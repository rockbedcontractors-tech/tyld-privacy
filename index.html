Privacy Policy for TYLD

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Rockbed Contractors, LLC ("we," "us," or "our") operates the TYLD mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

2. Data Collection and Use

TYLD operates primarily as an offline estimation and invoicing tool. Project details, change orders, and material calculations are stored locally on your device using an internal database. We do not collect, transmit, or sell personal or sensitive user data to external servers or third parties.

3. Device Permissions

The app may request access to your device's file storage solely for the purpose of generating, saving, and exporting PDF invoices and change orders locally.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
